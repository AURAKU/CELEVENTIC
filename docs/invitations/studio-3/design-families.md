# Invitation Experience 3.0 — Design Families

Configuration source: `src/lib/invitation/studio3/design-families.ts`

These families guide **future** `layoutVersion ≥ 2` / new-slug work. No catalogue visuals were changed.

## Priority reference experiences (build first after eligibility)

| # | Family | Creative direction | Opening defaults | Motion tone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `editorial-wedding` | Luxury editorial elegance, personalized guest journey | cinematic, monogram, envelope, classic-reveal | editorial |
| 2 | `brand-launch` | Modern product-launch storytelling, VIP RSVP | cinematic, classic-reveal, minimal-instant | launch |
| 3 | `memorial` | Respectful typography, subtle transitions, dignified storytelling | portrait, minimal-instant, classic-reveal | solemn |

Study Forever Afaris, Traditional Marriage, Femmora, and Memorial as **references only**.

## Additional families

- `heritage-ceremony` — contemporary heritage for traditional marriage (v2 only)  
- `celebration` — birthdays / milestones  
- `conference` — agenda / speakers / QR  
- `academic` — graduations (new SKUs OK)  
- `shower` — baby showers / naming  
- `custom` — organizer canvas (`custom-media`)

## Motion principles (all families)

- Intentional, skippable openings  
- `prefers-reduced-motion` → treat as `minimal-instant`  
- Never block RSVP / event facts behind unskippable animation  
- Memorial / solemn: no playful confetti, loud stingers, or forced audio
